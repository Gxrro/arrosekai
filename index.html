About Me

◇

Hey, I'm Arro-Sekai. I'm a music lover, cover artist, and creative person who somehow ended up doing a hundred different things and loving all of them.

I've been making music, writing ideas, creating visuals, and building small online worlds. Somewhere along the way, it turned into a place for covers, parodies, projects, and the occasional strange Tuesday afternoon experiment.

Outside of music, I'm a student, builder, and internet wanderer with a soft spot for cozy design and projects that feel personal.

This site is my digital living room. Look around, click things, stay a while.
