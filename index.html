Hello my name is Gabriel Arro, the founder of this website, Arro-Sekai. Welcome to my world.

I am an Information Technology graduate with interests in data analytics, digital marketing, music production, voice acting, and creative media. Born in the Philippines and currently based in Jacksonville, Florida, I enjoy building projects that combine technical knowledge with creative expression.
